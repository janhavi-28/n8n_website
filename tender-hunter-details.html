Purpose

  • Government and enterprise procurement portals publish hundreds of new tenders every day across platforms like GeM and CPPP. This automation continuously tracks and identifies relevant procurement opportunities the moment they go live, eliminating manual searches and ensuring your organization never misses a deadline.
  • Evaluating extensive tender specifications manually is time-consuming and often causes delays in decision-making. This system instantly checks scope requirements, eligibility criteria, and submission deadlines against your company profile so your team knows immediately which contracts are worth pursuing.
  • By taking over portal discovery and initial qualification, this automation frees bid teams from tedious administrative work, enabling your specialists to focus on proposal strategy, competitive pricing, and submitting winning tenders to drive business growth.
